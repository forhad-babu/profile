About Me

Aspiring Researcher,
seeking opportunity

I am Md. Forhad Babu, a final-year undergraduate student in Applied Chemistry and Chemical Engineering (ACCE) at the University of Rajshahi, Bangladesh, with a CGPA of 3.65/4.00. My academic and research interests lie at the intersection of sustainable energy, advanced materials, computational chemistry, and electrochemistry, with a particular focus on developing materials and understanding molecular-level processes for clean energy applications.

My primary research interests include Dye-Sensitized Solar Cells (DSSCs), Perovskite Solar Cells, battery and energy-storage technologies, and nanomaterials energy applications and Wastewater Treatment, Density Functional Theory (DFT), electrochemistry and corrosion science. I have developed practical experience with computational and scientific tools including Gaussian, GaussView, Materials Studio, CAPS1D, MATLAB, OriginPro, Data analysis with R and Python Programming, and I am particularly interested in applying computational approaches to complement experimental research and accelerate materials discovery.

Beyond academics, I have actively contributed to student-led academic and research communities through leadership and organizational roles. I currently serve as President of the Rajshahi University Higher Education and Research Forum (RUHERF) and President of the University Students' Association of Sariakandi-Sonatola (USASS), along with holding executive responsibilities in several other student organizations. These experiences have strengthened my leadership, communication, project coordination, teamwork, and ability to work effectively with diverse groups toward shared academic goals.

My long-term goal is to build a research career in sustainable energy and advanced materials, ultimately contributing to both academia and technological innovation. I am particularly motivated to pursue graduate study in an environment where I can work with leading researchers, gain advanced research training, and contribute to interdisciplinary projects addressing challenges in clean energy, energy storage, and materials innovation.

As a student from Bangladesh, access to advanced research facilities and international research environments is a significant challenge without financial support. Therefore, I am actively seeking a fully funded Master's or PhD opportunity abroad that will allow me to develop as an independent researcher while contributing meaningfully to my research group and the broader scientific community. I see graduate education not simply as the next academic step, but as an opportunity to transform my curiosity and research potential into impactful scientific contributions.

I am driven by the belief that science is most meaningful when it contributes to society. Through advanced education, collaborative research, and persistent learning, I aspire to contribute to the development of sustainable energy technologies and materials that can help address some of the world's most pressing environmental and energy challenges.
